# Gene tree estimation, in Lean

[![CI](https://github.com/formal-phylogenetics/lean4-phylo-gene-tree/actions/workflows/ci.yml/badge.svg)](https://github.com/formal-phylogenetics/lean4-phylo-gene-tree/actions/workflows/ci.yml)

Lean 4 formalizations of papers on estimating a phylogenetic tree from sequences under a model of
their evolution, typically from one alignment or locus.

This repository is a hub of inference in the collection rooted at
[lean4-phylogenetics](https://github.com/formal-phylogenetics/lean4-phylogenetics). It holds the
library [`PhyloGeneTree`](PhyloGeneTree), for what two or more of its papers share and the root
library does not hold. It has no modules yet; its [README](PhyloGeneTree/README.md) says what goes
there, and the catalog of the papers, [`catalog/papers.toml`](catalog/papers.toml), from which the
status and the checklist below are generated.

The collection's
[conventions](https://github.com/formal-phylogenetics/lean4-phylogenetics/blob/main/docs/CONVENTIONS.md) and
[workflow](https://github.com/formal-phylogenetics/lean4-phylogenetics/blob/main/docs/WORKFLOW.md)
are in the root. Each paper is formalized with the
[formalize-math-paper](https://github.com/vltanh/formalize-math-paper) skill: its results are proved
by the paper's own arguments, and an audit reports its errors and gaps.

## The papers

Each paper is in the family of its method, with the theory of that method. General results are in
the hubs of theory:
[lean4-phylo-combinatorics](https://github.com/formal-phylogenetics/lean4-phylo-combinatorics) for
the combinatorics of trees and compatibility,
[lean4-phylo-algebraic](https://github.com/formal-phylogenetics/lean4-phylo-algebraic) for
invariants and identifiability,
[lean4-phylo-statistics](https://github.com/formal-phylogenetics/lean4-phylo-statistics) for sample
complexity and consistency, and
[lean4-phylo-geometry](https://github.com/formal-phylogenetics/lean4-phylo-geometry) for spaces of
trees. Alignments are in
[lean4-phylo-alignment](https://github.com/formal-phylogenetics/lean4-phylo-alignment), species
trees in
[lean4-phylo-species-tree](https://github.com/formal-phylogenetics/lean4-phylo-species-tree), and
networks in [lean4-phylo-network](https://github.com/formal-phylogenetics/lean4-phylo-network). The
substitution models of family 3 go to the root library.

## Starting a paper

From [lean4-phylogenetics](https://github.com/formal-phylogenetics/lean4-phylogenetics), when the
paper's formalization starts:

```sh
python3 scripts/status.py next               # the open papers, by priority
python3 scripts/new_paper.py <id>            # creates ../papers/gene-tree/lean4-<id>
cd ../papers/gene-tree/lean4-<id> && claude  # then: /formalize-math-paper <the paper's link>
```

## Building

```sh
lake exe cache get
lake build
lake build Phylogenetics
lake env lean scripts/Audit.lean
```

Lean and Mathlib `v4.37.0-rc1`. [`lakefile.toml`](lakefile.toml) requires lean4-phylogenetics at the
commits of the train. The audit, [`scripts/Audit.lean`](scripts/Audit.lean), checks that every
declaration of this library and of the libraries it requires uses only Lean's standard axioms;
`python3 scripts/gen_audit.py` writes its `import all` lines. CI builds the library, runs the audit,
and checks that the status and the checklist above are current.
